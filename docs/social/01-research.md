# Background research

Research date: 4 October 2026. Public offers, repository evidence and a [12-post content-account audit](06-content-account-audit.md). This is not an Instagram grid, follower or account-performance audit.

## The business we are marketing

The repository at `/Users/benito/Documents/personal/blessed-moon` names the business Blessed Moon Studio. It is a Next.js 16.3 / React 19.2 / Tailwind 4 site with a dark terminal aesthetic, amber accent, ASCII moon, and shared motion system. Public routes include home, work, about and contact. `/system` is a hidden design reference. `src/lib/studio-data.ts` is the portfolio source; `src/lib/site-config.ts` identifies the current URL as `https://blessed-moon.vercel.app`.

Its documented audience is founders, CTOs and product leaders who value craft and clarity. The portfolio also supports a Brazilian business track: Oishi demonstrates turning a restaurant's actual offer into clear website content; F&A Móveis demonstrates a real catalogue connected to WhatsApp. Other projects include software and dashboards. Treat a prototype, pitch or ongoing project as such. Do not turn a functioning website into a claim of increased sales.

**Recommendation:** explain the business decision behind the work. A screen becomes evidence when a buyer understands the problem it solves. Position the new service as connecting the audience, content, website and measurement.

## Evidence and its limits

Sebrae's June 2025 article describes WhatsApp and Instagram as leading channels used by Brazilian entrepreneurs. Its headline and body contain different overall digital-selling percentages, so this plan does not reuse that figure. The finding supports a Portuguese Instagram test for local business owners; it does not establish their budget for a premium agency. [Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/cultura-empreendedora/celular-e-fonte-de-renda-para-pequenos-negocios-sebrae-tem-guia-e-cursos-para-orientar-sobre-uso-das-redes/).

The 2025 Edelman–LinkedIn report surveyed nearly 2,000 global professionals and discusses thought leadership's role in buying decisions. It supports testing practical decision stories on LinkedIn. It is not a Brazilian microbusiness study, a forecast of Blessed Moon's leads, or proof that a founder account always outperforms a company page. [Report publisher](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report).

Meta describes account-specific advice in Instagram's professional dashboard. Use that guidance and actual account insights to refine formats and cadence. Start with a sustainable schedule; no universal posting-time or follower-growth claim is justified by this research. [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/).

## Public offer comparison

These are observed advertised offers, checked on the research date. They are different markets and delivery models, not a representative price survey. Results and testimonials on their websites were not independently verified.

| Reference | Observed offer | Lesson for Blessed Moon |
|---|---|---|
| [Duna Design](https://dunadesign.com.br/consultoria-de-redes-sociais-preco-planos-pacotes/) | Brazil; advertised monthly tiers R$590, R$990, R$1,290, combining planning, creative and management | Broad low-price bundles exist. State research depth, deliverables and limits rather than competing on post count. Date and actual fulfilment of that rate card are unverified. |
| [CenoDigital](https://cenodigital.com/blog/social-media-agency-pricing-uk-2026/) | UK; publishes its own £750 / £1,200 / £2,200 monthly prices; ad spend separate | Website, brand and social can form a connected offer. Distinguish service fee from advertising budget. Its market-wide claims are not adopted here. |
| [Siesta Media](https://siestasocialmedia.com/pricing/) | Sarasota; $799 / $1,599 / $3,199 monthly tiers include filming and execution | Production location and footage ownership change the economics. Blessed Moon should not promise shoots merely because it offers planning. |
| [Designjoy](https://www.designjoy.co/) | Design subscription; public $5,995 price and one active request at a time | Useful adjacent packaging example: make capacity limits explicit. This is not a comparable social strategy rate. |
| [Superside](https://www.superside.com/social-media-creative) | Social creative production for scale-ups and enterprises; discusses audience, brand and visual delivery | Buyers can need creative production without needing account management. Keep those services distinct. |

**Inference:** a small studio can distinguish itself through a usable strategy, original case-based content, clear scope and website measurement. None of these sources proves demand at a particular Blessed Moon price; validate through discovery conversations and paid pilots.

Unimeet can illustrate the process only with permission and accurate attribution. Do not publish internal research, account credentials, private analytics, client correspondence or unapproved performance claims.

## Channel and positioning recommendation

Run a first eight-week pilot with LinkedIn primary for the existing founder/product positioning: two original core stories weekly, one adapted to Instagram in Portuguese. Both libraries contain 16 draft alternatives; this does not commit Benito to publishing 32 posts. Instagram explains local-business decisions; LinkedIn explains business constraints and product decisions. Reverse priority if Brazilian small businesses become the chosen market. The [platform selection](07-platform-selection.md) records the reasoning and review criteria. Founder-profile publishing is recommended for Benito's direct voice, not proven to outperform company pages. Confirm official accounts before launch.

Keep the site English initially, matching the existing brand. Portuguese social traffic creates a language mismatch worth measuring; a Portuguese landing page is a later choice if relevant enquiries justify it. Avoid automatic multilingual work or an entire repositioning before validating the audience.

Lead with: **Research-backed content. A clear path to enquiry.** Support it with tangible deliverables, a small number of original examples, and a straightforward contact action.
