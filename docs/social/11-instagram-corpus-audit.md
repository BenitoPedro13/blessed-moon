## Decisions for Blessed Moon

1. **Proof before decoration:** an actual portfolio capture should have a named
   buyer task and an explained choice. Oishi, F&A Móveis, Markado and Flora now
   illustrate different commercial/product workflows.
2. **Develop the middle:** carousels contain a concrete question, comparison,
   checklist, workflow and real proof/explicit worked example. Each slide has
   explanatory copy. A six-slide sequence is our pilot format, not a claim that
   six is an optimal length established by this sample.
3. **Make the action match the destination:** a case post leads to that case;
   a service explanation leads to the real service page; a brief leads to contact.
   No fictitious lead magnet, webinar, comment automation or promised outcome.
4. **Vary the composition:** dark, paper and amber surfaces; comparison, workflow,
   checklist, screenshot and closing-action layouts. The artwork uses our identity
   and original words, with no copied competitor campaign designs.
5. **Keep buyer and peer content separate:** the core plan answers commissioning
   questions, explains existing work and clarifies scope. Awards, recruitment,
   conference announcements, memes and design tutorials are not copied into it.
6. **Use channel fit as a test:** LinkedIn remains primary for founder/product-leader
   positioning; Instagram is the Portuguese visual-proof test for Brazilian business
   owners. Reverse that priority if that becomes the chosen market. This archive
   improves creative decisions but cannot rank channel conversion performance.

The website exposes ten account notes and twenty source examples. Each original
brief links relevant patterns separately from actual project evidence. The 16-story
queue is a useful starting library, not 356 repurposed captions or an automatic
research retrieval engine. Presets now include fuller explanations and broader
project examples. These changes create testable, more useful creative; their effect
on engagement or enquiries must be measured after publication.

## Capacity, measurement and pricing

Two original LinkedIn stories and one Instagram adaptation per week; start with
4–6 production hours and record actual time. Use real saves/shares, appropriate
conversations, tagged visits, qualified enquiries, proposals and production time.
The blank worksheet distinguishes missing data from observed zero. No benchmark
or sales projection is inferred from competitor appearance or follower counts.

The service remains **Social Media Strategy & Content Planning**. Proposed internal
fees remain R$3,900 once and R$1,800/month for strategy review, with the client
producing/publishing. Those are workload-based proposals, not a verified market
average. A 356-cover bespoke audit is this studio's own research, not an automatic
promise that every R$3,900 client project includes the same depth. Production,
publishing, community management, ads and custom website plan pages need separate
scope and capacity. Public copy uses a custom quote.

Remaining research gaps: smaller local studio references, verified target-buyer
interviews, account insights, complete selected case videos/carousels and real
commercial outcomes. These do not prevent review of the current plan; they define
what to validate during the pilot.
