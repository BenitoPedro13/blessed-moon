# Content-account audit

Inspected 4 October 2026. Scope: 12 publicly accessible LinkedIn posts across six
account families. Five agency comparators plus RD Station, a same-audience educator
and software vendor. Netguru includes one post from its co-founder. These are
qualitative references, not a representative sample or a list of confirmed bidding
competitors. Buyer overlap is inferred from positioning and case subjects; the
studio's priority market has not yet been validated.

## Method and limits

Record the subject/hook, visible format indicators, destination/CTA and an original
application. Public text, listed attachments and available transcripts were reviewed;
images/video were not comprehensively visually inspected. Only the named author's
post is attributed; unrelated recommended posts and comments are excluded. The public
pages use relative age labels and cached snapshots which vary across retrievals, so
exact publication dates and current cadence are not asserted. Several samples are
older references; inspection date does not mean publication date.

No follower, reaction or view totals are used to rank effectiveness. Account insights,
qualified enquiries, attributed revenue and paid distribution were unavailable. Any
case results remain the publisher's claims. Instagram-feed access was incomplete:
this is not evidence of Instagram carousel/Reel performance or competitor cadence.
Use account insights and a verified recent feed sample before claiming those findings.

## Decisions for Blessed Moon

1. Buyer education: translate a buyer question into clear information and a next step.
2. Project evidence: a named project plus a specific decision is more useful to test
   than an unexplained screenshot. It is not established here as higher performing.
3. Process: make tradeoffs legible without turning a technical post into a tutorial
   aimed only at other developers.
4. Service: show what the strategy contains and which production work needs another scope.

These observations support experiments, not guarantees. The original Oishi/F&A stories
use the studio's project records. No copied testimonial, result or competitor caption
becomes Blessed Moon evidence. Instagram briefs adapt these buyer questions; format
choice remains a test informed by Meta's account-specific dashboard guidance.

For a paid client project, select up to five relevant competitor/account references
within the agreed scope, include same-audience educators where helpful, and seek two
recent accessible posts per account. Document inaccessible content and sample dates;
never fill a gap with invented performance. Revise the sample after the buyer/market
is confirmed, including closer local or solo-studio comparators where available.
