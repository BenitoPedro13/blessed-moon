# Blessed Moon Studio: social strategy and new service

Updated 5 October 2026. Research supporting the website implementation; see docs/tasks/TASK-social-strategy-and-service.md for status.

Two related deliverables:

1. A strategy for marketing Blessed Moon itself, with Instagram and LinkedIn planning pages and a measurement plan.
2. A public service offering **Social Media Strategy & Content Planning**, with optional monthly strategy review. Production and account management are separate scopes.

Read [research](01-research.md), [agency content plan](02-agency-content-plan.md), [service and pricing](03-service-and-pricing.md), and [measurement](04-measurement.md). The implementation follows this packet and the task document.

The [Instagram corpus audit](11-instagram-corpus-audit.md) covers 356 distinct
records and saved covers from ten selected accounts, 59 captured full captions and
84 saved inner-slide images. Each account's role and limits are explicit. The
[earlier LinkedIn audit](06-content-account-audit.md) records the initial sample;
the live plan now retains ten posts across five families after Benito excluded
LayerUp and Sebrae profiles. The [platform selection](07-platform-selection.md)
explains the provisional channel recommendation. Each brief separates observed
patterns, original project evidence and proposed format tests.

The [review guide](05-review-guide.md) lists pages, changes and pending decisions.
A [prompt for a future website agent](08-agent-review-prompt.md) explains the
implemented scope and how to continue without duplicating it.
The [production workspace guide](09-production-workspace.md) explains the new
visual previews, planning/measurement graphs, post builder and local file workflow.

Provisional decisions (revised 5 October 2026): English leads for international founders and product leaders, as `@blessedmoon.studio` Instagram carousels and the same slides as LinkedIn documents on Benito's profile, two a week. An optional Portuguese Instagram account carries the Oishi and F&A Móveis stories for Brazilian businesses, one a week. See [platform selection](07-platform-selection.md) and `docs/tasks/TASK-social-copy-v3.md`.

No social account insights, current enquiry counts, client budgets, production capacity, or live Umami dashboard data were supplied. Recommendations are hypotheses, not measured Blessed Moon performance. Pricing is proposed internally, not approved for publication. Existing tracker code was inspected; production ingestion was not verified.

Benito is the sole operator; production volume and support must fit one person's available capacity. Implementation does not include deployment.
