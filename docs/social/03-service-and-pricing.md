# Service definition and proposed pricing

4 October 2026. Internal recommendations in BRL unless stated otherwise; not an approved public rate card or market average.

## Name and promise

**Social Media Strategy & Content Planning** / **Estratégia e Planejamento de Conteúdo para Redes Sociais**.

This describes research, positioning, channel choices, an editorial plan and measurement. **Social media management** describes the ongoing operational work of creating/publishing content and, when contracted, managing comments/messages. **Social media marketing** is the broader umbrella and can include paid campaigns.

Suggested public copy:

> Content with a reason to exist.
>
> We research your audience and competitive landscape, define what your channels should say, and turn it into a practical content plan. Clear briefs, a usable calendar, and measurement connected to your website help you decide what to publish and what to improve.

Public deliverables: audience and competitor research; positioning and content pillars; Instagram/LinkedIn recommendations when appropriate; a 30-day calendar; example briefs; a website measurement plan; a handover session. Say exactly what is included in the signed scope. Do not imply a custom software dashboard is included in every strategy project.

Public CTA: **Discuss your content strategy** → the existing contact flow, with Social Media Strategy available as a project type. Public pricing line: **Fixed project scope. Quote agreed before work begins.** This follows the site's existing custom-quote approach. Proposed numerical prices remain in the planning pages until approved.

These are recommended test offers derived from defined workload and positioning. They are not source-reported national averages. The strategy calendar contains ideas, not 12 completed designs. A carousel is one asset with a stated slide limit, e.g. six slides. A second-channel adaptation of the same idea is not a second original asset.

Do not advertise monthly production or full management before Benito confirms he wants to deliver it. Community handling, on-site filming, paid ads, media spend, influencer work, translations, daily Stories and custom dashboard development require separate scopes. Strategy and management should not be combined into one ambiguous low fee.

For international clients, test a separately scoped strategy quote of **US$1,200–2,000** only after understanding their brief and support expectations. This is a proposal, not a conversion of the BRL rate or an international market average. Keep original currencies when comparing external offers.

## A defensible price calculation

Use actual time and costs to confirm the quote. Example for the strategy package:

| Work | Budget |
|---|---:|
| Discovery and source gathering | 3 hours |
| Audience and five-competitor research | 6 hours |
| Positioning, channel plan and pillars | 4 hours |
| Calendar and four detailed briefs | 5 hours |
| Measurement plan | 2 hours |
| Handover and consolidated revision | 2 hours |
| Total | **22 hours** |

Illustrative economic assumptions: R$110/hour internal labour cost, R$100 direct tools/costs, 15% scope contingency, and 10% reserve for taxes/payment fees. These are modelling assumptions; substitute the agency's actual costs and tax treatment.

`floor = ((22 × 110 + 100) × 1.15) / (1 − 0.10) = R$3,220`

A R$3,900 quote leaves some margin above that planning floor. If a comparable project takes 35 hours, its floor under the same assumptions is about R$5,047; reduce scope or quote more. AI-assisted delivery can save time, but fact checking, editing, asset work, client communication and revisions remain real work.

Monthly review assumes approximately 6–10 hours; production approximately 20–28 hours, depending on asset complexity. Record actual hours on the first two engagements. Long carousels, filming and multi-language copy can invalidate those estimates. A R$500–800 monthly bundle is a different offer from a researched strategy plus custom creative delivery.

Commercial suggestions: 50% at strategy kickoff and 50% at handover; monthly support paid at the start of its month; client approval deadlines; one consolidated revision; explicit change requests for added channels or deliverables. These are business recommendations, not a drafted contract or legal advice.

## What would change the recommendation

Audience, niche, number of channels/languages, asset complexity, client approval cycles, data availability, access needs and service capacity. Before publishing fixed fees, confirm what can be delivered profitably. The base strategy service is the closest match to the work already done for Unimeet.
